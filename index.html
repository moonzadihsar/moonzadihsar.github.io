About me

Engineering better decisions.

I am a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at Mississippi State University. I earned my Ph.D. in Industrial & Systems Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 2026.

My research focuses on addressing complex decision-making problems using operations research, network optimization, and data-driven approaches, with applications in supply chains, healthcare systems, and security. I am particularly interested in how decision-making changes when networks are concealed, uncertain, or adaptive.

My teaching connects analytical foundations to real-world engineering decisions through examples, optimization models, and interactive demonstrations.

Research

Three connected research directions.

Developing decision-analytics frameworks to understand complex networks, allocate resources, and design effective interventions.

01 / INFERENCE

Understanding hidden supply networks

Combining production pathways, bill-of-materials information, and trade data to study potential connections between legitimate and illicit supply chains.

Supply NetworksData Integration
02 / HARM REDUCTION

Healthcare resource allocation

Studying inventory sharing and transshipment decisions to improve access to critical resources such as naloxone under uncertain, intermittent demand.

Inventory OptimizationDeep RL
03 / INTERVENTION

Intervention in adaptive networks

Optimizing disruption and protection strategies in multiplex networks that may propagate, recover, or adapt in response to interventions.

Network OptimizationDecomposition
